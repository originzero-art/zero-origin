# zero-origin // Project Governance & Fiscal Transparency

This document outlines the governance model, financial transparency mechanisms, and decision-making structures governing the `zero-origin` project and its parent initiative, `originZero.Art`.

---

## 1. Project Leadership & Engineering Stewardship

- **Founder & Lead Architect:** Sebastian Paul Banszky ([banszky.men](https://banszky.men)), Senior Network Security & Cloud Architect.
- **Engineering Operations:** Audited European engineering operations based in Romania, operating in full compliance with European Union regulations.
- **Architectural Scope:** The lead architect retains technical steering authority over core protocol specifications, dual-licensing demarcation, and cryptographic invariants to ensure long-term architectural coherence and security.

---

## 2. Fiscal Hosting & Open-Ledger Financial Transparency

To ensure complete public accountability for institutional grants and community contributions, the project operates under a strict fiscal hosting model:

- **Fiscal Host:** Transitioning to **Open Collective Europe** (OCE, Brussels) as the non-profit fiscal host.
- **Public Ledger:** 100% of incoming grant disbursements, public donations, and operational expenses are recorded on an open, public ledger accessible to anyone worldwide.
- **Audited Execution:** Engineering services, infrastructure costs, and R&D milestone deliverables are lawfully invoiced and audited under European fiscal standards.

---

## 3. Creator Advisory & Human Pilot Group

`originZero.Art` and `zero-origin` exist to serve human creativity, not speculative tech platforms:

- **Founding Artist Cohort:** The project is guided by an active pilot group of human creators currently exhibiting on the platform (fine-art painters and visual identity designers).
- **Advisory Input:** Creator feedback directly informs edge defense behavior, ensuring that security filtering never impedes legitimate art collectors, galleries, or human audiences.
- **Sovereign Independence:** Creators maintain 100% exclusive copyright and ownership over all their creative assets, with zero platform sales commissions.

---

## 4. Decision-Making & Contribution Model

- **Technical Proposals:** Major architectural changes, crawler detection heuristics, or specification amendments must be submitted as RFC documents via GitHub Issues / Pull Requests.
- **Review Criteria:** All proposals are evaluated against the three core invariants:
  1. Does it preserve the strict dual-licensing boundary (AGPLv3 for daemon / Apache 2.0 for specs)?
  2. Does it maintain 100% self-hosted execution without introducing centralized SaaS dependencies?
  3. Does it protect human creators without compromising legitimate web discoverability?
- **Consensus Process:** Technical discussions occur publicly in English on GitHub, with final sign-off executed by the lead architect.
